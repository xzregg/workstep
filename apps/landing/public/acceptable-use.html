WorkStep Acceptable Use Policy
可接受使用政策

Updated / 更新日期：2026-11-06

English

This policy describes acceptable use of WorkStep's AI workflows and official services, including prompts, AI-generated code and text, other outputs, and their distribution. Use only data, projects, tools and accounts you are authorized to access. Follow applicable law and the terms and safety policies of connected model and tool providers.

Prohibited uses

  • Do not use WorkStep's AI workflows to generate, transform or distribute sexually explicit or NSFW content, pornography, sexual exploitation, or sexual content involving minors. This prohibition includes code, text and workflows intended to produce or facilitate such content.
  • Do not facilitate illegal activity, fraud, scams, impersonation, exploitation of minors, human trafficking, or instructions intended to cause physical harm.
  • Do not generate or deploy malware, ransomware, phishing, credential theft, destructive code or tools for unauthorized access. Security testing must be authorized by the owner of the systems involved.
  • Do not collect, expose or misuse personal data, confidential files, credentials or private communications without authorization. Do not use workflows for doxxing, stalking or unlawful surveillance.
  • Do not produce targeted harassment, threats, hateful abuse, deceptive spam, or content that infringes intellectual-property or other rights.
  • Do not bypass provider safety controls or access restrictions, or disguise prohibited activity through another engine, workflow step or output format.

Review and responsibility

You are responsible for your inputs, permissions, execution and use or sharing of outputs. Review generated code and text for safety, accuracy, privacy and rights before running or publishing them. AI outputs may be incorrect or unsafe. A configured workflow does not establish permission to access a system or publish content.

Reports and official services

Report suspected misuse to the support address below with a brief description and, where available, a public URL. Do not send identity documents, home addresses, passwords, API keys, private conversations or private project files. Redact personal information from any supporting material.

WorkStep may restrict or suspend access to official services for violations of this policy, subject to applicable law. This statement does not claim that local workflows are centrally monitored or that all prohibited outputs can be detected. The software remains licensed under Apache License 2.0; this policy does not amend that license or remove rights granted by it.

简体中文

本政策说明 WorkStep 的 AI 工作流与官方服务的可接受使用范围，涵盖提示词、AI 生成的代码与文本、其他产物及其传播。请仅使用你有权访问的数据、项目、工具与账号，遵守适用法律以及所连接模型和工具供应商的条款与安全政策。

禁止用途

  • 禁止使用 WorkStep 的 AI 工作流生成色情、露骨性内容或 NSFW 内容，或对这些内容进行转换、传播；禁止性剥削及任何涉及未成年人的性内容。此禁令也涵盖旨在生成或促成上述内容的代码、文本与工作流。
  • 禁止协助违法活动、欺诈、诈骗、冒充他人、剥削未成年人、人口贩运，或提供旨在造成身体伤害的指引。
  • 禁止生成或部署恶意软件、勒索软件、钓鱼内容、凭据窃取或破坏性代码，以及用于未授权访问的工具。安全测试必须获得相关系统拥有者的授权。
  • 禁止未经授权收集、披露或滥用个人数据、保密文件、凭据或私人通信；禁止人肉搜索、跟踪或非法监视。
  • 禁止定向骚扰、威胁、仇恨辱骂、欺骗性垃圾信息，或侵犯知识产权及其他权利的内容。
  • 禁止绕过供应商的安全控制或访问限制，或通过更换引擎、工作流步骤或输出格式掩饰被禁止的活动。

检查与责任

你应对输入、权限、执行行为及产物的使用或分享负责。运行或发布前，须人工检查生成代码与文本的安全性、准确性、隐私与权利问题。AI 产物可能错误或不安全。配置工作流不代表获得系统访问或内容发布授权。

举报与官方服务

如发现涉嫌滥用，请联系下方支持邮箱，提供简要说明，以及已有的公开链接。不要发送身份证件、家庭住址、密码、API 密钥、私人对话或私有项目文件；支持材料中的个人信息须先脱敏。

对于违反本政策的行为，WorkStep 可依法限制或暂停相关官方服务。本说明不代表集中监控本地工作流，也不保证检测出全部违规产物。软件仍适用 Apache License 2.0；本政策不修改该许可证，也不取消其授予的权利。
